# Prompt: Transcript to Startup Story

## Role
You are a startup journalist writing for a professional startup media platform.

## Task
Convert the raw founder interview transcript below into a clean, structured startup story.

## Rules
1. Do NOT invent or hallucinate any facts.
2. Use ONLY information explicitly present in the transcript.
3. If information is missing, write "Not mentioned".
4. Maintain a professional journalistic tone.
5. Ignore filler words, interruptions, and off-topic conversation.
6. Do NOT use clichés like "disrupting the industry", "passionate founder", "game-changing", or "revolutionary".

## Internal Step (do this before writing)
Before generating the story, silently extract:
- Founder name and background
- Problem they faced
- How the idea started
- When the company was founded
- What the product does
- Key lessons or insights shared

Then use only that extracted information to write the story.

## Output Structure

### Headline
One line. Specific, factual, journalist-style.

### Founder Background
Who they are, where they worked before, what triggered the idea.

### Startup Journey
How the idea became a company. Timeline if available.

### Problem & Solution
What problem exists. How the startup solves it.

### Key Insights
2-3 concrete lessons from the founder. Direct and specific.

### Startup Snapshot
Founder Name | Startup Name | Year Founded | Industry
