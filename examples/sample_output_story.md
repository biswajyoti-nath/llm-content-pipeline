## Headline
From Swiggy Engineer to DevTools Founder: How QuickScale Automates Cloud Scaling for SaaS

---

## Founder Background
Rohan spent 2023 as a backend lead at Swiggy, where he routinely dealt with the pressure of manually scaling infrastructure during high-traffic events — cricket matches, sales, and app surges. The repeated frustration of a problem that should have been automated became the seed of QuickScale.

---

## Startup Journey
Rohan began building an automation script on weekends as a personal fix. When he shared early versions with developer friends at other companies and found they faced identical problems, he recognized a market signal. QuickScale was formally registered in January 2024.

---

## Problem & Solution
Engineering teams at SaaS companies lose time and stability managing cloud infrastructure manually during traffic spikes. QuickScale automates the scaling process, removing the need for engineers to intervene during high-demand periods.

---

## Key Insights
1. Talk to users before writing a single line of code.
2. Pricing DevTools requires careful strategy — developers are cost-sensitive, but value must be justified to finance teams. QuickScale got pricing wrong twice before finding a model that worked.
3. The best startup ideas come from problems the founder has personally experienced, not problems read about secondhand.
